Overlap Analysis

Measures how much of one route layer's volume (for example, the UAM corridors) lies inside other layers (for example, REH, REA or airport-flow envelopes). Volumes are the boxes drawn in the 3D view (width and height of the start waypoint, centered on the segment, altitude interpolated along it). The plan is divided into square cells; in each cell the altitude ranges of each layer are merged, so lanes and routes that share a waypoint segment are counted once. A segment is the pair of waypoints it joins, with all lanes and routes on it; segments touching a vertiport or airport are approach segments and are reported apart.
