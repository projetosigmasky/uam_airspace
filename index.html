Overlap Analysis

Checks the 3D volumes of one route layer (for example, the UAM corridors) against other layers (for example, REH, REA or airport-flow envelopes). Each segment is the box drawn in the 3D view: the width and height of its start waypoint, centred on the segment, with altitude interpolated along it. Counts are distinct segments, identified by their two waypoint names; segments touching a vertiport or airport are approach segments.
